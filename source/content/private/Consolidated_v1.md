### 7.6 Key Drills Reference

| Drill | How | What It Fixes |
|---|---|---|
| **Towel drill** | Towel 2" behind ball on rug; hit ball without touching towel | Ball-first contact, descending strike |
| **Brush drill** | No ball; half-swings brushing rug AFTER where ball would be | Low point in front of ball |
| **Tee drill** | Tee at ball, second tee 2" in front; clip front tee | Down and through, not scoop |
| **Step drill** | Start on back foot; step forward as downswing begins | Hip-initiated sequence |
| **Punch shot drill** | Ball back 2", weight 70% forward, 3/4 swing, low finish | Anti-chunk, weight forward |
| **3:1 drill** | Slow backswing (3-count), normal downswing (1-count) | Sequence and tempo |
| **Gate drill (putting)** | Two coins 4" apart, 6" in front of ball; putt through gate | Face angle and path at impact |

### 7.7 Swing Fundamentals

- **Sequence:** Hips → Torso → Arms → Club. Always.
- **Core principle:** Hit down to make it go up. The iron lifts it. Never scoop, never flip.
- **Setup:** Feet inside shoulders; ball just forward of center (irons); "bar stool posture"; grip Vs point at right ear; body lines parallel-left of target.
- **Impact:** Hips ~45° open; weight 70% forward; ball-first contact; descending strike.
- **Finish:** Hold 3 seconds. If you can't, you swung too hard.

### 7.8 3-Month Plan to Proficiency

| Month | Focus | Key Milestone |
|---|---|---|
| Month 1 (Weeks 1–4) | Contact & Short Game | Ball-first contact 7/10 short irons; complete first 9-hole round |
| Month 2 (Weeks 5–8) | Direction & Distance Control | Ball starts toward target; chip and 2-putt; long irons airborne |
| Month 3 (Weeks 9–12) | Consistency & Course Management | Pre-shot routine; play 18 holes; write down score |

**Proficiency Target at 3 Months:** 9 holes ~54–63; 18 holes ~108–126; 2-putt from inside 20 ft most of the time; keep pace with group ahead.
