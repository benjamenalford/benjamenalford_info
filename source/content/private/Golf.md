## II. THE GRIP

**Interlock or overlap. Neutral. In the fingers.**

- **Left hand:** Grip runs from middle of index finger to heel pad. 2–2½ knuckles visible. V points at right ear. Thumb slightly right of center.
- **Right hand:** Lifeline fits over left thumb. Grip in the fingers. V points at right ear. Thumb slightly left of center.
- **Pressure:** 4–5 out of 10. Firm but not tight. Last three fingers of left hand, middle two of right hand do the holding. Thumbs and index fingers are relaxed.
- **Check before every swing.** It takes 3 seconds.

## III. THE SWING FUNDAMENTALS

### Setup (Address)
- Feet inside shoulders, ball just forward of center (irons)
- Hips back like sitting on a stool — "bar stool posture"
- Arms hang naturally from shoulders
- Grip Vs point at right ear
- Body lines (feet, hips, shoulders) parallel-left of target — like railroad tracks
- Clubface square to target — leading edge perpendicular to target line

### The Sequence
**Hips → Torso → Arms → Club.** Always. The step drill teaches this.

1. **Backswing:** Hips rotate ~45°, shoulders ~90°. Right arm bends ~90°. Left arm stays extended. Club goes up.
2. **Transition:** Hips start rotating toward target. Arms stay back. This creates lag.
3. **Downswing:** Hips continue clearing. Arms swing down and deliver the club.
4. **Impact:** Hips ~45° open. Clubface square. Right arm straightening. Ball-first contact. Descending strike.
5. **Follow-through:** Hold finish 3 seconds. If you can't, you swung too hard.

### The Core Principle
**Hit down to make it go up. The iron lifts it. Not you.** Never scoop. Never flip. The club's loft sends the ball upward when you strike it with a descending blow.

### The Anti-Chunk Fix
- Weight forward at impact (70% on front foot)
- Ball-first contact — the towel drill teaches this
- Accelerate through the ball — decelerating causes chunks
- Punch shot drill: ball back 2 inches, weight forward, low finish — makes chunking almost impossible

## IV. THE DAILY TRAINING PLAN (15 MINUTES)

### Phase 1: Dynamic Warm-Up (4 minutes)

| Exercise | Reps | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Hip swings (front-to-back) | 5 each leg | Hip rotators |
| Hip openers (gate swing) | 5 each leg | External rotation |
| Overhead squats | 5 reps | Hip mobility + thoracic extension |
| Cross-body shoulder pulls | 5 each side | Shoulder mobility + thoracic rotation |
| Side bends + rotation (with club) | 5 each side | Thoracic spine — the #1 limitation |
| Shadow swings (slow, no ball) | 5 reps | Arm-hip dissociation, sequence |

### Phase 2: Dumbbell Strength (6 minutes)

| Exercise | Weight | Sets × Reps | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goblet squat | 5 lb → 8 lb | 3 × 8 | Hip mobility, glute power, stability |
| Single-arm dumbbell row | 8 lb | 3 × 8 each arm | Back strength, arm-body connection, grip |
| Dumbbell woodchop | 5 lb | 3 × 6 each direction | Rotational power, hip-initiated sequence |

### Phase 3: Swing Practice (5 minutes)

| Drill | Time | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Punch shot drill (10 reps) | 1 min | Weight forward, ball-first, can't chunk |
| Towel drill + pillow backstop (half-swings, moderate speed) | 2 min | Descending strike, ball-first contact |
| 3:1 slow-motion swings (3/4 backswing) | 1 min | Full sequence — hips lead, arms deliver |
| Chipping into pillow (3/4 swings, real tempo) | 1 min | Listen for the click — proper contact |

### Phase 4: Putting (2 minutes — maintain, don't neglect)

| Drill | Time | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Gate drill (2 coins, 4" apart, 6" in front of ball) | 1 min | Face and path control |
| Start-line drill (aim at a shoe/cup/mark) | 1 min | Starting the ball on line |

## V. THE KEY DRILLS — REFERENCE

| Drill | How | What It Fixes |
|---|---|---|
| **Towel drill (modified)** | Towel 2" behind ball on rug. Hit ball without touching towel. Half-swings into pillow backstop. | Ball-first contact, descending strike |
| **Brush drill** | No ball. Half-swings brushing the rug AFTER where the ball would be. | Low point in front of the ball |
| **Tee drill** | Tee where ball goes, another tee 2" in front. Clip the front tee. | Down and through, not up and scoop |
| **Step drill** | Start on back foot only. Step forward as you start downswing. Hips trigger, arms follow. | Hip-initiated sequence |
| **Punch shot drill** | Ball back 2", weight 70% forward, 3/4 swing, low finish. | Anti-chunk, weight forward, commitment |
| **3:1 drill** | Slow backswing (count 1-2-3), normal downswing (count 1). | Sequence and tempo |
| **Gate drill (putting)** | Two coins 4" apart, 6" in front of ball. Putt through the gate. | Face angle and path at impact |
| **Start-line drill (putting)** | Aim at a target. Focus only on starting the ball on that line. | Directional control |

## VI. THE 3-MONTH PLAN TO PROFICIENCY

**"Proficient" for a 45-year-old beginner means:** consistent ball-first contact, minimal chunks/tops, reliable short game, able to play 9 holes without embarrassment, keeping pace, and enjoying yourself. Not scratch golf. Functional golf.

---

### MONTH 1: CONTACT & SHORT GAME

**Goal:** Make clean contact with every iron. Build the foundation.

| Week | Focus | Range Session (2–3x/week) | Home Practice (daily 15 min) | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | First range visit. No expectations. | 1 session. PW and 7-iron only. Half-swings. Punch shots. Find the click. | Full daily routine. Emphasis on punch shots and towel drill. | Make contact. Any contact. |
| **Week 2** | Ball-first contact. Descending strike. | 2 sessions. PW, 9-iron, 7-iron. Half to 3/4 swings. Towel drill at range (put a tee 2" behind ball, don't hit the tee). | Full daily routine. Add putting — 5 min on rug after the 15 min. | 7 out of 10 shots are ball-first (no chunks) |
| **Week 3** | Full swings with short irons. | 2 sessions. PW through 7-iron. Start with half-swings, progress to full when contact is consistent. | Full daily routine. | Full swings with PW and 9-iron produce consistent, airborne shots |
| **Week 4** | Introduce mid-irons. First 9-hole round. | 2 sessions. Add 6-iron and 5-iron. Still mostly short irons. **Play 9 holes** — forward tees, pick up after 6 strokes, no pressure. | Full daily routine. | Play 9 holes. Finish every hole (or pick up at 6). Don't keep a real score yet. |

**Month 1 checkpoints:**
- ✅ Ball-first contact 7/10 times with short irons
- ✅ Ball gets airborne consistently
- ✅ Basic grip, setup, and sequence are automatic
- ✅ Completed one 9-hole round without quitting

---

### MONTH 2: DIRECTION & DISTANCE CONTROL

**Goal:** Start aiming. Learn where the ball is going. Build the short game.

| Week | Focus | Range Session (2–3x/week) | Home Practice (daily 15 min) | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Week 5** | Aim and face control. | Pick a target every shot. Notice where the ball goes relative to the target. If it's right, check grip and face at address. | Same routine. Add gate drill emphasis in putting. | Ball starts within 20 yards of target (short irons) |
| **Week 6** | Chipping and pitching. | Spend 50% of range time on short game. PW chips from 10–30 yards. Focus on bump-and-run — land it just on the green, let it roll. | Towel drill + pillow = chipping practice. Add distance putting (lag putts across the rug). | Get up and down from 20 yards at least 2/10 times |
| **Week 7** | Introduce long irons / 3-wood. | Add 4-iron and 3-iron (or 3-wood if you acquire one). These are harder — accept fewer good shots. Focus on smooth tempo, not distance. | Same routine. | Hit 4-iron and 3-iron airborne at least 4/10 times |
| **Week 8** | Play 9 holes with intention. | 1 range session + **1 nine-hole round**. Play from forward tees. Track how many fairways and greens you hit (even if the number is low). | Same routine. | Complete 9 holes with no triple bogeys (pick up rule still applies) |

**Month 2 checkpoints:**
- ✅ Ball starts roughly toward the target most of the time
- ✅ Can chip and 2-putt from off the green (up and down occasionally)
- ✅ Long irons are functional (not pretty, but airborne)
- ✅ Lag putting leaves ball within 5 feet most of the time

---

### MONTH 3: CONSISTENCY & COURSE MANAGEMENT

**Goal:** String together good shots. Think your way around the course. Start scoring.

| Week | Focus | Range Session (2–3x/week) | Home Practice (daily 15 min) | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Week 9** | Shot routine. Every shot gets the same routine: pick target → check grip → address → one practice swing → go. No standing over the ball frozen. | Apply routine to every range ball. | Same routine. Add putting routine (read → practice stroke → go). | Every shot has a consistent pre-shot routine |
| **Week 10** | Course management. | **Play 9 holes.** Strategy: aim for the center of every green, never short-side yourself, take your medicine from trouble (punch out sideways, don't try the hero shot). | Same routine. | Play 9 holes with zero penalty strokes from trying hero shots |
| **Week 11** | The driver (if you get one). | If you've acquired a driver, start hitting it. If not, keep teeing off with 3-wood or 3-iron. Either is fine. | Same routine. | Find a reliable tee shot — even if it's a 3-iron that goes 150 yards straight |
| **Week 12** | **Assessment round.** | **Play 18 holes for the first time.** Forward tees. No expectations. Just play golf — one shot at a time, pick up when needed, enjoy being outside. | Same routine — you've earned it. | Complete 18 holes. Count every stroke. Write it down. That's your starting point. |

**Month 3 checkpoints:**
- ✅ Consistent pre-shot routine on every shot
- ✅ Can play 9 holes without losing more than 2 balls
- ✅ Short game saves at least 2–3 strokes per round
- ✅ Three-putts are rare (1–2 per 9 holes max)
- ✅ Completed 18 holes

## VII. THE PROFICIENCY TARGET — 3 MONTHS

By the end of Month 3, you should be able to:

| Skill | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Ball-first contact** | 8/10 shots with short irons, 6/10 with long irons |
| **Direction** | Ball starts within 15 yards of target most of the time |
| **Chipping** | Get the ball on the green and within 10 feet from 20 yards out |
| **Putting** | 2-putt from inside 20 feet most of the time. Lag putt from 30+ feet to within 5 feet |
| **Course play** | Complete 9 holes in under 2 hours. Complete 18 in under 4.5 hours |
| **Scoring** | 9 holes: ~54–63 (6–7 over par per hole average). 18 holes: ~108–126 |
| **Pace** | Keep up with the group ahead |
| **Etiquette** | Fix ball marks, replace divots, don't walk on lines, yell fore |

**That's proficient.** That's a golfer. Not a great golfer — a functional one who can play a round, enjoy it, and not hold anyone up. Everything after this is refinement.

## IX. RANGE SESSION TEMPLATE

Every range session should follow this structure:

| Step | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Warm up | 5 min | Dynamic warm-up (same as home routine) |
| 2. Short irons | 15 min | PW, 9-iron. Start with punch shots and half-swings. Progress to full swings. Pick a target every shot. |
| 3. Mid irons | 10 min | 7-iron, 6-iron, 5-iron. Full swings. Pick a target. |
| 4. Long clubs | 10 min | 4-iron, 3-iron (or 3-wood/driver when you have one). Smooth tempo. Don't swing harder — swing smoother. |
| 5. Short game | 10 min | Chipping area or putting green. PW chips from 10–30 yards. Bump-and-run. |
| 6. Putting | 5 min | Lag putts from 20–30 feet. Short putts from 3–5 feet. Gate drill if you have coins. |

**Total: ~55 minutes.** Not 200 balls. Not 2 hours. Focused, structured, done.

## XIII. THE TERMS YOU NEED RIGHT NOW

1. **Grip** — in the fingers, 2–2½ knuckles, Vs at right ear, pressure 4–5
2. **Address** — setup before the swing
3. **Descending strike** — hit down to make it go up
4. **Ball-first contact** — hit the ball before the ground
5. **Sequence** — hips → torso → arms → club
6. **Slice** — ball curves hard left-to-right (your likely miss)
7. **Chunk / Fat** — hitting the ground before the ball
8. **Thin / Top** — hitting the ball with the leading edge
9. **Follow-through** — hold your finish 3 seconds
10. **Pure** — the perfectly struck shot. You'll know it when you hit it.

The rest of the glossary will come naturally as you play.

## XIV. THE MENTAL GAME

- **Golf is not cycling.** Effort ≠ result. Trying harder often makes it worse.
- **One shot at a time.** The last one is over. The next one doesn't exist yet.
- **Your worst enemy is expectations.** You will be terrible at first. Every golfer was.
- **The 7-iron is your reset button.** When everything falls apart, grab it and hit half-swings until you find contact.
- **Leave the driver in the bag when you're struggling.** 3-iron off the tee in play > driver in the woods.
- **A proper iron shot takes a divot.** After the ball. That's not a chunk — that's correct.
- **The ball doesn't care about your feelings.** It responds to physics. Give it the right physics and it'll fly. Give it the wrong physics and it won't. Simple as that.
