## NOTES

- Custom Campagnolo retrofit: Super Record 11 crank + Chorus 10 drivetrain
  + Record brakes. A mixed-tier but coherent Italian build on an
  American aluminum frame.
- BSA threaded BB (68 mm) — clean for service, Campy Ultra-Torque compatible.
- 54 cm frame with 90 mm stem: the shorter stem compensates for the
  longer top tube (vs. Masi's 53/100 mm). Net reach may be similar,
  but the 54's longer TT + higher saddle = much more aggressive drop.
- Saddle height (76.0 cm) is 6.1 cm higher than the Masi (~69.9 cm).
  This may reflect saddle shape differences (Rival 143 vs. Concor) or
  a genuinely higher position. The 11.5 cm drop is the key comfort
  delta — nearly double the Masi's estimated drop.
- 40 cm bars are 2 cm wider than the Masi's 38 cm and wider than
  ideal for owner's shoulder width (~37–39 cm seam-to-seam).
- 172.5 mm cranks are 2.5 mm longer than the Masi's 170 mm. For
  spinal/foot-drop constraints, the shorter cranks on the Masi may
  be more comfortable (less hip closure at top of stroke).
- Campagnolo Eurus wheels with G3 spoking — a distinctive Campy
  wheelset, good match for the Italian group.
- To complete this card: measure top tube (C-C), seat tube (C-C),
  and reach (saddle nose to stem bolt) for direct geometry comparison
  with the Masi.
