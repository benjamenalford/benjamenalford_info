## NOTES

- Faliero signature + clamp-on cable guides + absence of brazed-ons →
  early-1970s Italian build, c. 1972–1975.
- Campagnolo Record groupset (Pat. 74) purchased separately from frame
  — not original to the Masi. However, the 1974 patent date makes the
  group era-correct for the frame (est. c. 1972–1975). This is a
  coherent period-correct retrofit, not an anachronism.
- 52/42 chainrings are period-standard (modern standard is 53/39 or
  compact 50/34). The 42 inner ring is slightly easier for climbing
  than a 39, which suits the bike's relaxed, sustainable character.
- Mavic GP4 rims are classic box-section French endurance rims —
  durable, comfortable, and correct for the era. Confirm whether
  tubular (sew-up) or clincher, as GP4 was produced in both formats.
  Most GP4s in this era were tubular.
- To confirm exact frame year: pull fork, check steerer for date
  stamp (format: "573" = May 1973). Check BB shell for +GF+ mark.
- Campagnolo clamp-on TT cable guides are the tiebreaker vs.
  early-1980s brazed-on era.
- Frame is a keeper — irreplaceable provenance. Not a replacement
  candidate for any modern bike.
